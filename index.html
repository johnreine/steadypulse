SteadyPulse

A steady pulse on your wrist.

SteadyPulse listens to your heart rate for a minute, then plays a gentle, steady tap on your Apple Watch, set a little below your heart rate. As your heart rate eases, the pulse eases with it.

Now in beta · Coming soon to the App Store

How it works

  1. Open it on your watch. SteadyPulse starts by itself and listens to your heart rate for one minute.
  2. Feel the pulse. A calm rhythm begins on your wrist, a few beats per minute below your average heart rate.
  3. Ease down together. If your heart rate settles, the pulse follows it down. It never speeds up.

Made to stay out of the way

One look, one tap

Big numbers and big buttons. Your heart rate and the pulse rate are on the first screen.

Yours to tune

Choose how far below your heart rate the pulse sits, the lowest rate it will go, a fixed manual rate, and the feel of each tap.

Quiet by design

With Silent Mode on, the pulse is something you feel, not something you hear.

Private

No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Nothing leaves your devices. Privacy policy

Requirements

Apple Watch with watchOS 26 or later, paired with an iPhone running iOS 26.5 or later.
