# SteadyPulse Privacy Policy

_Effective October 29, 2026_

SteadyPulse is an Apple Watch app, with a small iPhone companion, that plays a
gentle, steady pulse on your wrist paced to your heart rate. It is made by an
independent developer, John Reine ("we" and "us" below). This policy explains
what information the app uses and what happens to it.

## The short version

**SteadyPulse does not collect any information about you.** It has no accounts,
no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It does not connect to the internet, and it
never sends your data to us or to anyone else. Everything the app uses stays on
your devices.

## Health data

With your permission, SteadyPulse uses Apple Health (HealthKit) on your Apple
Watch to:

- **Read your heart rate** while a session is running, so it can set the pace of
  the pulse on your wrist. Readings are used on the watch as they arrive. The app
  keeps about the last minute of readings in memory to work out an average, and it
  does not store them anywhere.
- **Save each session as a Mind and Body workout** in Apple Health, so it appears in
  your Health timeline.

SteadyPulse does not sell, share, or use your health data for advertising or
marketing, and none of it leaves your devices. Sessions saved to Apple Health are
kept by Apple Health, not by us; Apple handles that data, including any syncing
through iCloud, under Apple's own privacy policy.

You can turn SteadyPulse's access to Apple Health on or off at any time on your
iPhone in **Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → SteadyPulse**. Sessions it
saved can be deleted in the Health app.

## Your settings

The choices you make in the app, such as how far below your heart rate the pulse
sits, the lowest rate it will go, and the feel of the pulse, are saved on your
watch only. Deleting the app deletes them.

## What we don't collect

- No name, email address, or account of any kind
- No location
- No contacts, photos, or other personal information
- No usage analytics, advertising identifiers, or crash-reporting tools
- No third-party code or services

## Information Apple may share with us

Apple, not SteadyPulse, may share some information with app developers, and only
if you choose to:

- **Crash reports and usage statistics**, if you have turned on "Share with App
  Developers" in your device's Analytics & Improvements settings.
- **TestFlight beta information**, if you test a pre-release version through
  Apple's TestFlight. Apple shows us details such as the email address you were
  invited with (if you were invited by email), which builds you installed, crash
  reports, and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send.

We use this only to fix problems and improve SteadyPulse, and we never sell or
share it. Apple's privacy policy covers how Apple collects it.

## Children

SteadyPulse is not directed at children under 13, and it does not collect
information from anyone.
