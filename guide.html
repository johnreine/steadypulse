How to Use SteadyPulse

SteadyPulse has six screens. They sit one below the other, like pages in a stack. This guide shows each one, from the top down.

Before you start

  1. Wear your watch snugly. It needs to touch your skin to read your heart rate.
  2. Turn on Silent Mode. Press the long flat button below the round dial. Tap the bell so it turns red. Now you will feel the taps without hearing them.
  3. Open SteadyPulse. Press the round dial to see your apps, then tap this heart:

The first time, your watch asks if SteadyPulse may read your heart rate. Please allow it.

Moving between screens

To go down to the next screen: slide your finger up the watch screen, or turn the round dial on the side.

To go back up: slide your finger down, or turn the dial the other way.

The little dots on the right side of the watch show which screen you are on.

If you don't touch the watch for 30 seconds, it goes back to Screen 1 by itself.

Screen 1 of 6

Your heart rate

The big number is your heart rate: how many times your heart beats in one minute. BPM means “beats per minute.”

SteadyPulse starts by itself when you open it. The words at the bottom tell you what it is doing:

  • Listening 0:42 It is listening to your heart. The clock counts down one minute.
  • Buzz 67 You feel gentle taps on your wrist, 67 each minute. That is a little slower than your heart.
  • Manual 60 Manual taps are on, 60 each minute. See Screen 3.
  • Buzz off The taps are stopped.

If your heart rate goes down, the taps slow down with it. They never speed up.

Start and Stop

While the taps are on, you see one big Stop button. Tap it to stop the taps.

While the taps are off, you see two buttons:

  • Start Listens to your heart for one minute, then starts gentle taps a little slower than your heart.
  • Manual Starts taps right away, at the speed you set on Screen 3.

The taps keep going when you lower your arm. Come back here and tap Stop when you are done.

Screen 3 of 6

Manual BPM

This sets the speed of Manual taps: how many taps in one minute. It starts at 60, which is one tap each second.

Tap + for faster. Tap − for slower.

If Manual taps are already on, the speed changes right away. If the taps are off, you feel a sample tap each time you press.

Screen 4 of 6

Offset

This sets how much slower than your heart the taps are.

With 5, a heart rate of 72 gets 67 taps each minute. A bigger number means slower taps.

Most people can leave this at 5.

Screen 5 of 6

Minimum

This is the slowest the taps will ever go.

With 60, the taps never go slower than 60 each minute, even if your heart rate is lower.

Most people can leave this at 60.

Screen 6 of 6

Pulse

This sets how each tap feels.

Tap the arrows < > to try another. There are eight to choose from, such as “Strong triple tap” and “Heartbeat: firm, then light.”

If the taps are off, you feel each one as you choose it. SteadyPulse remembers your choice.

That's every screen

To get back to Screen 1, slide your finger down or turn the dial the other way. Or just wait 30 seconds.

If you see two dashes

The watch hasn't read your heart rate yet. Make sure your watch is snug on your wrist, and wait a few seconds. SteadyPulse keeps listening until it reads your heart.
